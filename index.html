FAQ

Straight answers

Do we need a new website?

Not always. We recommend a rebuild only when it materially improves the connected system. If your site is in good shape, we assess connecting to it instead.

Does this replace our existing software?

No. This is not a ServiceTitan replacement, and it does not replace your accounting, scheduling or invoicing tools. It connects to how you already work.

Is automated customer texting included?

No. It is not part of the current base implementation and is not operating. Review and reminder messages are prepared by the system and sent manually. Compliant automated messaging can be added later as a separate add-on.

Can you guarantee leads, reviews or search rankings?

No. We do not guarantee leads, revenue, reviews, reactivated customers, or placement in Google or AI assistants. We build the foundations that help. Placement still varies by query, user and location.
