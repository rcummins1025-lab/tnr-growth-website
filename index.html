FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a CRM?

No. A CRM is software you have to learn, fill in and keep up. We hold your customer book for you, track every lead by source, and show you who’s due. You don’t manage a system. We run it.

Do I have to learn software?

No. We install it, set it up and run it. You get one Today screen that shows who’s due, with the text already written. You read it and tap send.

What do you need from me?

Your customer list in whatever form it’s in today: a spreadsheet, an export from your invoicing tool, or a stack of old invoices. Access to your calendar. And one short kickoff call. We do the rest.

How long until it’s live?

About two weeks. In week 1 we load your customer book. In week 2 we connect your calendar and tracking number and it goes live. After that we run it for you every month.
