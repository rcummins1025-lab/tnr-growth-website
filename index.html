FAQ

Straight answers

Do you replace our existing software?

No. We don’t replace your accounting, scheduling, or invoicing tools. ServiceMomentum focuses on getting found, capturing and tracking leads, earning reviews, and bringing past customers back. It connects to how you already work.

Do we need a new website?

Often, yes. A conversion-focused, connected website is usually the cleanest foundation. But it depends on what you have today, which we assess before recommending anything. If your current site is in good shape, we’ll assess whether we can connect to it instead of rebuilding.

Is this a CRM?

It includes a private customer database and pipeline, but it is not a full CRM, scheduling, invoicing, or dispatch platform. It’s a focused customer-growth and retention system.

Is automated customer texting included?

No. Automated customer texting is not part of the core system and is not currently operating. Today, review and reminder messages are prepared by the system and sent manually (copy-and-send). Compliant automated customer messaging can be added later as a separate add-on. Separately, T&R Growth runs an opt-in operational SMS lead-alert program for authorized business recipients only. See our SMS Consent page. Your customers are never enrolled or messaged.

Can you guarantee Google or Gemini placement?

No. Anyone who guarantees it is overpromising. We build the foundations that help: accurate business information, useful content, structured data, crawlability, and consistency. Placement in search and AI assistants still varies by query, user, and location.

Who owns the website and customer data?

You do. It’s your website and your customer information. We design, install, and operate the system for you.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on scope and typically takes a few weeks. We agree on the timeline with you up front rather than promising a one-size-fits-all number.
