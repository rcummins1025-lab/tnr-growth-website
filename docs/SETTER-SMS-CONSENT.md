# Setter rule: consent before any text (owner outreach)

Internal note for anyone calling owners for T&R Growth LLC (Service Momentum).
This is what `sms-consent.html#owner-outreach` and our A2P campaign say we do.
If we do anything else, the published pages become false and the campaign can
be suspended.

## The rule

**Call first. Text only after a yes. Log every yes.**

1. Call the business's listed number. Never text a number first.
2. Ask: "Can I text you a 2-minute demo link?"
3. A clear yes on the live call counts as consent. So does the owner texting
   **DEMO** to your number. A maybe, a voicemail or a gatekeeper does not count.
4. Log the consent **before** you send the text:

   | Field | Example |
   | --- | --- |
   | Date | 2026-11-04 |
   | Time (with time zone) | 10:42 ET |
   | Team member | Ryan |
   | Phone number texted | the number the owner agreed to |
   | How | verbal on call / texted DEMO |

5. The first text confirms the request, names you and T&R Growth LLC
   (Service Momentum), and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." For example:

   > Hi Dana, Ryan at Service Momentum (T&R Growth LLC). Here's the demo link
   > you asked for on our call: https://tnrgrowthagency.com/... Reply STOP to
   > opt out.

## No, STOP and HELP

- **No on the call, or "don't contact me":** add the number to the shared
  do-not-contact list. Don't text it.
- **STOP (or stop, unsubscribe, cancel, quit, end):** stop texting that number
  right away from **every** team number, and add it to the do-not-contact list.
  Don't text it again unless the owner texts you first to restart.
- **HELP:** reply with who we are, hello@tnrgrowthagency.com, and how to opt out.
- Before every first text, check the do-not-contact list.

## Also

- Text only between 8am and 9pm in the owner's local time.
- No purchased lists. No bulk tools. Every text is typed by a person.
- Keep consent logs for at least four years (the federal time limit for TCPA
  claims), and keep the do-not-contact list for good. They're our proof if a
  carrier or anyone else asks.
