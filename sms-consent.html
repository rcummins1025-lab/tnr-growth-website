SMS Consent

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This page explains how T&R Growth LLC ("T&R Growth," "we," "us," or "our") collects consent for its internal SMS (text message) alert program. It is provided for information and transparency only. This page is not a sign-up form and does not enroll anyone. There is nothing to submit here, and no way to opt in from this website.

What the program is

T&R Growth operates a small, low-volume SMS program used only for internal operational alerts — for example, notifying our own team when a booking request comes in for a client we serve. The only recipients are authorized members of the T&R Growth team. Website visitors, prospects, clients, and customers are never enrolled and never receive these messages.

How we collect consent (email only)

Consent is collected only through direct email. No paper form, printed form, public website form, QR code, or text keyword is used, and there is no opt-in form anywhere on this website. The process is:

  1. Before an authorized team member's mobile number is added to the program, T&R Growth emails that team member the SMS program disclosures (what the program is, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, that they can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help, and that carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages).
  2. The team member replies from their own email account with an affirmative statement: “I agree to receive T&R Growth internal booking alerts at [mobile number].”
  3. T&R Growth retains that affirmative email reply as the record of consent, and only then is the number added to the program.

Consent to receive these messages is never a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Message frequency, rates, and opt-out

Message frequency varies and depends on booking activity — a participant receives a message when a relevant booking request is submitted. Message and data rates may apply. A participant can reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for assistance. Help is also available by email at hello@tnrgrowthagency.com.

How we handle mobile information

We will not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Message delivery is subject to carrier conditions, and carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contact us
